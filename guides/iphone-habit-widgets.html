Habit widgets on iPhone

Mostly Daily has widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy, and controls for Control Center. One tap checks a habit in without opening the app, and a second tap undoes it.

To add a widget, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Mostly Daily.

Home Screen

Free has three widgets. Habit is one habit with a ring to tap; pick a Limit habit and it gets a square button that logs a clear day. Next up shows the next open habit and, when everything is done, your mosaic. Today, the medium widget, has the day ring and your habits for the day.

Premium adds Today and week (large and extra large), a Month calendar (small and large), This week (medium and large, with up to 3 or 7 habits you choose) and Who you're becoming, the mosaic of your check-ins. Every widget works in the tinted and clear Home Screen styles.

Lock Screen and StandBy

On the Lock Screen there is a round day ring with done out of due, a rectangle with your next open habit, and a line above the clock like "2/5 · Read". The round Habit widget checks a habit in without unlocking the phone. In StandBy the widgets drop their background and the names get bigger, so you can read them from the bedside.

Control Center, Siri and Shortcuts

Add Check in to Control Center for a habit you build, or Clear day for a habit you want less of (Clear day asks you to unlock first). Siri understands "Check in Read in Mostly Daily" and "Rest today for Read in Mostly Daily", and none of the shortcuts open the app.

Private habits stay private

A Limit habit is never named on the Lock Screen; it shows as "a private habit". Turn on Keep private for any habit and every widget, control and notification shows "Private habit" with a plain symbol. Inside the app you still see the real name.
