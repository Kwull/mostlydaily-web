Sick days, travel and holidays in a habit tracker

In Mostly Daily you mark the day as Low energy or Travel, or set time off for a whole trip. Those days are not misses and don't lower your level.

Most habit trackers know two states, done and not done. A week with the flu then looks like seven failures, and two weeks at the sea look like you gave up. You need a way to tell the app that this day doesn't count.

One day: Low energy, Rest or Travel

Touch and hold a habit on Today and pick Rest today, Low energy or Travel. The day is skipped: it doesn't count for the habit or against it. Low energy is the one for days when you are ill or worn out, and you can use it as often as you need.

Rest and Travel share a soft budget of 4 days a month per habit, which you can change from 0 to 10. Going over is fine: the menu shows how many you used, and an extra rest day is still a rest day, never a miss. Each week that ends with the habit at Often or Mostly also earns one more rest day, and you can bank up to 4.

A trip or a holiday: time off

For more than a day or two, go to Settings, Time off. Choose the dates, a reason (Vacation, Travel, Low energy or Other) and either all your habits or only some of them. Reminders go quiet, those days never count as a miss and they don't use your rest days. The reason stays on your iPhone.

If you did it anyway

A check-in on a rest day or during time off still counts. A short walk on holiday is a done day.

Coming back

If you haven't opened the app for 3 days or more, it says welcome back and asks you to pick one habit for today, in its two-minute version if it has one. There is no list of what you missed. More in rest days are part of the plan.
