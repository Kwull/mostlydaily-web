How it works

Three small steps, then it mostly runs itself.

1

Start with who you want to become

A reader, someone who moves every day, someone who drinks mindfully. Pick a habit that votes for it.

2

Write when and where

"I will read 10 pages after dinner, on the sofa." Add a two-minute version for busy days: it counts fully.

3

Check in with one tap

From the app, a widget, the Lock Screen, Siri, or let Apple Health check it in for you.

Levels, not fragile streaks

Every habit grows through four levels. Mostly is the top: you never need 100%.

Forgiving by design

Your level dips gently after a miss and recovers just as fast. Your run only ends after two misses in a row: never miss twice.

Rest is rest

Rest, sick and travel days are real days, not misses. Plan time off for a holiday, and come back to a calm welcome, not a list of misses.

Build and Limit habits

Yes/no, counts or minutes for the habits you build. A colour calendar of clear, light and heavier days for the ones you want less of, with clear-day goals and runs.

Who you're becoming

Every check-in is a vote. Watch your identity mosaic grow one tile at a time, and look back with a weekly reflection and a monthly recap you can share.

Questions

What happens when I miss a day?

Nothing is reset. Your level dips a little and comes back just as fast when you check in again. Your run of "never missed twice" only ends after two misses in a row.

What is a Limit habit?

A habit you want less of, like drinks, sugar or screen time. You log the amount each evening and get a colour calendar of clear, light and heavier days, with a goal of clear days per week.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Your habits stay on your devices and sync through your own private iCloud.

What does Free include?

3 habits (one can be a Limit habit), levels, rest days, reminders, widgets, Apple Health and iCloud sync, with no time limit.

What does Premium add?

Unlimited habits with Focus mode, the evening check-in on the Lock Screen, every past reflection and recap, more widgets and app icons, and several reminders per habit.

What happens if Premium ends?

Nothing is deleted. Habits beyond the Free plan are paused with all their history, and you can export everything at any time.

Is my data private?

Yes. Mostly Daily has no server, no analytics and no tracking. Drinks you link to Apple Health stay in Apple Health and are never stored in iCloud.

Is it a treatment or recovery program?

No. Mostly Daily is for self-tracking and gives no medical advice. If you're worried about your health or drinking, please talk to a professional.
