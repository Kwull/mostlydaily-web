Two kinds of habit

Habits you build. Habits you want less of.

Build

Yes or no, a count, or minutes. Write when and where ("after dinner, on the sofa"), and keep a two-minute version for busy days: it counts fully. Apple Health can check in steps, exercise, mindful minutes, sleep and water for you.

Limit

Drinks, sugar, screen time. Log the amount in the evening and get a colour calendar: clear days, lighter days, heavier days. Set a goal of clear days a week. No judgement, just the picture.

Who you're becoming

Every check-in is a vote.

A reader. Someone who moves. Someone who drinks mindfully. Each check-in lands one tile in the habit's colour. Tiles are never taken away: a miss leaves no hole, because a tile is something you did, not a day you were judged on. Every hundred tiles, a mosaic is finished and kept.

Each week, Reflect tells you about your week in kind words, habit by habit.

How it works

Start small. It mostly runs itself.

  1. Add a habit

    Type a name, or pick one: Read, Move, Meditate, Floss, Alcohol-free days.

  2. Put a widget on your Home Screen

    Check in from there: one tap, done.

  3. Miss a day now and then

    Your level dips a little and comes back. Just don't miss twice.

No account

No sign-in, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your habits stay on your devices and sync through your own iCloud.

Questions

Questions

What happens when I miss a day?

Nothing is reset. Your level dips a little and comes back just as fast when you check in again. Your run of "never missed twice" only ends after two misses in a row.

What is a Limit habit?

A habit you want less of, like drinks, sugar or screen time. You log the amount in the evening and get a colour calendar of clear, lighter and heavier days, with a goal of clear days per week.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Your habits stay on your devices and sync through your own iCloud.

Is my data private?

Yes. No server, no analytics, no tracking. Check-ins from Apple Health and the drinks you log stay on your phone and are never put in iCloud.

What happens if Premium ends?

Nothing is deleted. Habits beyond the Free plan are paused with all their history, and you can export everything at any time.

Is it a treatment or recovery program?

No. Mostly Daily is for self-tracking and gives no medical advice. If you're worried about your health or drinking, please talk to a professional.
