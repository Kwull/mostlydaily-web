Why Mostly

Aim for Mostly, not perfect.

Streak apps count days until you miss one. Mostly Daily measures how often you show up, and never asks for 100%.

Mostly

Four levels. Mostly is the top.

Starting, Sometimes, Often, Mostly. There is nothing above Mostly, so there is nothing to lose.

Never miss twice.

Rest

Rest is a real day.

Rest, low energy and travel days are not misses. Finish a week at Often or Mostly and you earn an extra rest day.

Two kinds of habit

Habits you build.
Habits you want less of.

Build

Start tiny. Step up when it feels easy.

Yes or no, a count, or minutes. Keep a two-minute version for busy days; it counts fully. When a step has felt routine for a few weeks, the app offers the next one. Nothing changes by itself.

  • Apple Health can check off steps, workouts, sleep, water and more
  • Write when and where: “after dinner, on the sofa”

Limit

A calendar of clear days, not a count of days since.

Drinks, sugar, screen time. Log the amount in the evening and get a colour calendar: clear, lighter, heavier. Set a goal of clear days a week. No judgement, just the picture.

  • Note what a day was about: Social, Unwinding
  • Set up your space with up to three small steps
  • Self-tracking only. It gives no medical advice.

Questions

Honest answers.

What happens when I miss a day?

Nothing is reset. Your level dips a little and comes back just as fast when you check in again. Your run of "never missed twice" only ends after two misses in a row.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Your habits stay on your devices and sync through your own iCloud.

What if I hit the 3-habit limit on Free?

Free has 3 habits, and Premium has unlimited habits with Focus mode keeping 5 in focus at a time. When a new habit would go over, a note on the form says so before you add it, and offers "Pause a habit…". Pick one to pause: its history and level are kept, it waits under Paused, and you can resume it any time. Nothing is deleted.

What happens if Premium ends?

Nothing is deleted. Habits beyond the Free plan are paused with all their history, and you can export everything at any time.

Can I change an earlier day?

Yes. On a habit's calendar you can change today and the six days before it, and every earlier day stays readable. Premium (and the 2-week trial) lets you change any earlier day too. Earlier days look faded with a small lock on Free, and nothing is ever removed.

Is it a treatment or recovery program?

No. Mostly Daily is for self-tracking and gives no medical advice. If you're worried about your health or drinking, please talk to a professional.
