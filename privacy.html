Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Mostly Daily collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Where your data lives

  • On your devices: your habits, check-ins and settings.
  • Your private iCloud: habits, check-ins for Build habits and time off sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. We can't read it.
  • This device only: check-ins for Limit habits (for example alcohol-free days), unless you turn on sync for that habit.
  • Apple Health (optional): if you allow it, Mostly Daily reads activity, mindfulness, sleep and water data to check habits in for you, and can save the drinks you log as Number of Alcoholic Beverages. Health data is never stored in iCloud by the app and is never sent to us.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Your choices

Export everything as CSV, or delete all data in Settings. Removing the app removes its data from the device; data in your iCloud can be deleted in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date.
