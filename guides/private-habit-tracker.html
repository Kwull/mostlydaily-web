A private habit tracker with no account

Mostly Daily has no account, no sign-in and no server. Your habits sync through your own private iCloud, health data stays on your iPhone and in Apple Health, and there are no analytics or ads.

A habit tracker knows a lot about you, like when you sleep and how often you drink. That is a good reason to keep it off a company server. The App Store privacy label for Mostly Daily says Data Not Collected.

Where each thing is kept

Your habits, the check-ins you make yourself and your time off sync through your private iCloud, so a new iPhone gets them back. The developer can't read them. Check-ins that come from Apple Health stay on the iPhone, and each device reads its own Health. Drink logs stay in Apple Health or on the iPhone and never go to iCloud. Other Limit habit logs stay on the iPhone too, unless you turn on Sync logs through iCloud for that habit. The reason you give for time off and the Your day list never leave the device.

Without iCloud the app works the same and keeps everything on the iPhone. Settings shows where your data lives.

Nothing in the app talks to a server

There is no sign-in, no e-mail, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. Data leaves the iPhone only through your own iCloud, Apple Health's own sync, Apple's purchase system and files you share yourself.

Private on the Lock Screen

Habits you want less of are never named on the Lock Screen. Turn on Keep private for any habit and widgets, Control Center, notifications and Siri show "Private habit" instead of its name. See habit widgets on iPhone.

Take it with you

Settings, Export and import makes CSV files of your habits, check-ins and time off on any plan. Premium never locks your data, and Delete all data removes your habits, check-ins and time off from the iPhone and from iCloud. The full details are in the privacy policy.
