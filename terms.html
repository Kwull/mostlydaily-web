Premium

  • Premium is available as a yearly subscription (with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers), a monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
  • When Premium ends, your data stays yours: habits beyond the Free plan are paused with their history, and export always works.

Mostly Daily is for self-tracking. It does not give medical advice and is not a treatment or recovery program. If you are worried about your health or drinking, please talk to a professional.
