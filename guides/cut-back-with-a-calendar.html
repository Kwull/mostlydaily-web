How to cut back on drinks, sugar or screen time

Count clear days. Set a goal like 4 clear days a week, log the amount each evening, and look at the month as a colour calendar.

A clear day is a day with none. Four of them a week is a real change, and over a month you can see it on the calendar. A week with three clear days still counts for three.

Set it up as a Limit habit

In Mostly Daily, add a habit and pick "Do less" at the top, or start the name with "Less" or "Stop". Choose a unit (drinks, treats, minutes, or your own word), how much still counts as a light day (2 by default) and how many clear days a week you aim for (4 by default). For screen time, use minutes for one app or one kind of use, such as social media, and log the minutes yourself.

Log it once, in the evening

When the day is nearly over, one tap on the square button logs a clear day, or you enter the amount. The Habit widget and the Clear day control in Control Center do the same without opening the app. You can add one reason to a day, like Social or Unwinding, and the habit shows a count per reason. It describes what happened and gives no advice.

Read the calendar

Each day gets a tile: green for clear, amber with one dot for light, coral with two dots for heavier, and a moon for a rest day. Days with nothing logged stay empty and are never counted as a slip. The card on Today says how the week is going, for example "3 of 4 clear days this week". A light day only means it wasn't a clear day, and after a heavier day the app says "Tomorrow is a fresh day."

Drinks and Apple Health

Drinks can be kept in Apple Health. The app reads drinks other apps logged and saves the ones you log there. Drink logs stay in Apple Health and on your iPhone, never in iCloud. Read more in habits you want less of.

Mostly Daily is for self-tracking. It gives no medical advice and is not a treatment or recovery program. If you are worried about your health or your drinking, please talk to a professional.
