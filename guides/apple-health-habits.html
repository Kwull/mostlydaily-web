Habits that check themselves off from Apple Health

Link a habit to Apple Health and Mostly Daily checks it off when the day reaches your goal: 8,000 steps, 30 minutes of exercise, a run, 7 hours of sleep, 2 litres of water.

Your iPhone and Apple Watch already count a lot of what you do. Ticking the same thing off by hand is one more chore.

What a habit can follow

A habit you build can follow steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes, workouts, walking and running distance, cycling, swimming, sleep hours, stand hours or water. For workouts you choose the kind, like a run, yoga or a swim, or any workout. The app shows only sources that fit the goal: a habit in minutes can follow exercise or a workout, and a habit in steps can follow steps.

Four more check off wellbeing habits: Close my rings (or just the Move ring), Get outside (time in daylight), In bed by 23:00 (from your sleep) and Check in with how I feel (a State of Mind entry). The app only sees whether the day counted. It never shows calories, ring amounts or what you logged as a mood.

How the check-in happens

When you open the app, and in the background when Health has new data, Mostly Daily reads the last week for each linked habit and checks off the days that reached the goal. Part of a count or timed goal still counts as showing up. Anything you log by hand wins: a rest day you set stays a rest day.

Ideas from Apple Health

Not sure what to track? Tap Ideas from Apple Health when you add a habit. The app looks at your last 30 days on the iPhone, suggests habits you already do, and keeps nothing from the scan.

Your Health data stays on the iPhone

Access is optional and asked per habit. Check-ins that come from Apple Health stay on the iPhone and never go to iCloud, and each device fills them in from its own Health. Apple Health works on the Free plan. For drinks, see how to cut back on drinks, sugar or screen time.
