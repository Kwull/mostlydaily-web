Check-ins

Tap the ring on a habit to check in; tap again to undo. Touch and hold the ring for the two-minute version, rest, sick or travel. For a habit you want less of, tap the square to log a clear day, or tap Log to enter the amount. To change a past day, open the habit and tap the day in its calendar.

Habits

Tap Edit on Today to reorder, pause or archive habits. The + button adds a habit: type a name or pick a suggestion, and tap More options for the full form. Today, Reflect and Settings are the three tabs.

When does a day end?

At 3:00 by default, so a late night still counts for the day it started. Change it in Settings > Day ends at.

Widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget and search for Mostly Daily. Tap the ring (or the square, for a habit you want less of) on a widget to check in: it fills right away, without opening the app. When every habit is done, the Next up widget shows your mosaic. Widgets move to the new day on their own when your day ends.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize and add Mostly Daily. For Control Center, open it, tap + and add Check in, Clear day or Close the day. If a widget ever looks out of date, open the app once.

Reminders

Each habit can have a reminder (Premium: several). Settings > Reminders gives the overview. You get at most two a day. If a habit's reminders are ignored three times in a row, they pause until you check in again.

Apple Health

When you create a habit that can use Apple Health (steps, exercise, mindful minutes, sleep, stand hours, water, workouts, drinks), “Use Apple Health” is on; the system asks once when you save. Check-ins that come from Health stay on this phone. Settings > Apple Health lists what is read and saved; manage access in the Health app > Sharing > Apps > Mostly Daily.

iCloud

Habits, the check-ins you make yourself and time off sync through your private iCloud. Check-ins from Apple Health and drink logs stay on each phone; a second phone fills those days from its own Apple Health. Other Limit habit logs stay on the phone unless you turn on sync for that habit.

How the levels work

Settings > About > How Mostly works explains the levels, never missing twice and rest days in the app's own words.

Premium

Restore purchases on the Premium sheet (Settings > Mostly Daily Premium). Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. When Premium ends, habits beyond the Free plan are paused with all their history; nothing is deleted.

Export and delete

Settings > Export and import saves your habits, check-ins and time off as CSV files, on any plan. Settings > Delete all data removes everything from the app and your iCloud.
