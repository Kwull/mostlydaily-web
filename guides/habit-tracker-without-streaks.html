A habit tracker without streaks

Mostly Daily has no streak counter. Each habit has a level from Starting to Mostly, and one missed day never resets anything.

A streak counts days in a row, so one missed day sends it back to zero. That day usually comes when life is already hard, like a cold or a child up all night. The next morning the app says you are starting over, and for a lot of people that is the day they stop.

Why streaks backfire

After a few weeks the goal moves from doing the habit to protecting the number. People do a rushed version at midnight just to keep it alive, or tick it off when they didn't do it. When the streak breaks, the drop from 120 to 0 says those 120 days didn't count. They did, and the habit is still mostly there.

What Mostly Daily counts instead

Each habit has a consistency level based on how often you showed up over the last couple of months: Starting, Sometimes, Often and Mostly. Mostly is the top level and you never need 100%. A miss after a good month costs a few points, and they come back as fast as they went.

Next to the level is a run: the days since you last missed twice in a row. One miss keeps the run going. Two misses in a row end it, and a new run starts with your next check-in. More on that in why one missed day doesn't break a habit.

Days that don't count either way

Rest, low energy and travel days are not misses. They don't help the level and they don't hurt it. If you are ill or away, see sick days, travel and holidays.
